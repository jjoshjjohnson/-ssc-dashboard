## DISCOVERY CALL FRAMEWORK

### Pre-call (5 min research):
- Company size + industry
- Public pain signals (slow website, manual-looking forms, LinkedIn posts about overwhelm)
- Estimated automation opportunity

### Call structure (20 minutes):
1. (3 min) Context: "Tell me what your week looks like"
2. (8 min) Pain discovery: "What's the most manual thing you do?" / "How many hours?"
3. (5 min) Automation opportunity: Describe what we'd build (specific to their pain)
4. (4 min) Close: "I can send you an automation audit proposal — $500, credited toward retainer if you sign within 30 days. Want me to send it today?"

### Objections:
- "Too expensive" → "What's an hour of your time worth? This pays back in week 1."
- "We tried tools before" → "You tried tools. We build custom automation — different category."
- "Not sure it'll work for us" → "That's exactly what the $500 audit is for — we prove it before you commit."
- "Need to think about it" → "Of course. I'll follow up Friday. What would help you decide?"
