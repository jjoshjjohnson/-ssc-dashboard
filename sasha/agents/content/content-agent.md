## VOICE & TONE STANDARDS

- **Audience:** US/EU SMB founders, ops managers, marketing directors
- **Tone:** Confident, specific, ROI-obsessed — no buzzwords
- **Format:** Short sentences. Active voice. Numbers whenever possible.
- **Avoid:** "leverage," "synergy," "cutting-edge," "AI-powered" (overused)
- **Use:** "automated," "saves X hours," "generates $X," "replaces manual"
